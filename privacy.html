Privacy Policy for Enve Book Player

Effective Date: 05/01/2026

1. Introduction

Enve Book Player ("the App") is an audiobook and ebook reader that connects to user-provided, self-hosted services. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

2. Information We Collect

a. Personal Information

The App does not collect, store, or share personal information, including:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number

b. User Content and Server Data

All books, metadata, and progress data remain on your server or your device. The developer does not have access to this data.

c. Usage Data

The App does not collect analytics, tracking data, or usage metrics.

3. How Information Is Used

No personal or usage data is collected, processed, or shared. All functionality occurs locally or between your device and your configured server.

4. Third-Party Services

The App does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

5. Data Storage and Security

  • Progress may be stored locally on your device
  • Server credentials are stored securely on your device
  • No data is transmitted to the developer

6. Children’s Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated. Changes will be reflected by updating the Effective Date.
