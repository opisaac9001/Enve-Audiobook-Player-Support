Privacy Policy for Enve Book Player Effective Date: 05/01/2026 1. Introduction Enve Book Player (“the App”) is an audiobook and ebook reader that connects to user-provided, self-hosted services. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App. 2. Information We Collect a. Personal Information The App does not collect, store, or share personal information, including but not limited to: Name Email address Phone number b. User Content and Server Data The App allows you to connect to your own self-hosted servers (such as personal library servers). All books, metadata, and progress data remain: On your server, and/or Stored locally on your device The developer of Enve Book Player does not have access to this data c. Usage Data The App does not collect analytics, tracking data, or usage metrics. 3. How Information Is Used Because no personal or usage data is collected, your information is not processed, stored, or shared by the developer. All functionality occurs: Locally on your device, or Directly between your device and your configured server 4. Third-Party Services Enve Book Player does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. If you connect the App to external or self-hosted services, those services operate independently and are governed by their own privacy policies. 5. Data Storage and Security Reading and listening progress may be stored locally on your device Server credentials entered into the App are stored securely on your device No data is transmitted to the developer’s servers 6. Children’s Privacy The App does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. Since no personal data is collected, the App is suitable for general audiences. 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the “Effective Date” above. 8.
